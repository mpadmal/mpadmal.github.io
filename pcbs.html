PSLab Mini

FOSSASIA · Wi-Fi pocket instrument

Dual-MCU host-offload architecture: STM32H563 (Cortex-M33) host plus ESP32-C3 (RISC-V) wireless co-processor over UART/SPI. 2-channel 12-bit oscilloscope at 5 MSPS with BNC inputs, logic analyzer, and multimeter, streaming over Wi-Fi/UDP.

Magic e-Paper

FOSSASIA · battery-less NFC e-ink writer

Drives a 24-pin FPC e-paper display from a bitmap received over NFC-V (ISO 15693) or USB-C, built around an ST25DV dynamic NFC tag. Fully battery-less: powered by RF energy harvested from the 13.56 MHz field through a tuned antenna. Designed in KiCad.
